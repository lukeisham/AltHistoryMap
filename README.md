# AltHistoryMap

An app made for my son Solomon.

A zoomable world map for building alternative histories: create imaginary maps, flags and crests, and save them. Found cities, plant flags and paint territory, or start from one of the ready-made empire sketches and change it.

## Open it
Visit [althistorymap.pages.dev/app](https://althistorymap.pages.dev/app/) in a current browser. It is also at [lukeisham.github.io/AltHistoryMap](https://lukeisham.github.io/AltHistoryMap/). Nothing is sent anywhere: your map stays on your computer.

## Use it
- Pick a starting map from **Scenarios** at the bottom, or begin with the blank world.
- **Political**, **Province** and **Culture** each show one layer of the map. Keys 1, 2 and 3 switch between them.
- Switch to **Edit** to paint, fill, draw borders and place cities, flags, symbols and labels. Undo with Ctrl/Cmd-Z.
- Zoomed fully out only the country colours show. Zoom in for flags, then country names, then capitals and symbols, then every other city. A country's smaller areas get their name only once you are zoomed in to the capitals. Use the Colony tool (key O) for a triangle marker. Every pop-up has a **×** to close it, and a colour's hover text is its country's name.
- **Cities by nation** colours each city's dot by the nation that holds it. **Country names** shows or hides each country's name over its land on Political.
- **Save** downloads your map as a file; **Open** (or dropping the file on the page) loads it again. **PNG** saves a picture.
- **Flags** are drawn in the flag designer: pick any colour with the **+** swatch, or stamp a whole coat of arms, or just its shield with **Shield only**, onto the flag. Rest the pointer on a flag to see it larger.
- **Crests** are built in the crest maker from shield shapes, divisions, ordinaries and charges, with an animal on either side of the shield like a coat of arms. Each animal can face the shield or away, and the crest can have a mound under it, a motto ribbon and a crown above the shield. A soldier can stand in place of an animal. Both open from the panel on the left in Edit mode, and both have **Save PNG**.
- Rest the pointer on any heraldic or flag word in either designer to see what it means.
- Every colour is a country. Drag a crest from the Crests list onto land or a colour to give it to that country. Double-click a colour, press **Key facts**, or click land in View mode to see its flag, crest, philosophy, ruler, government, Latin motto, currency and demonym. Pick a square on the philosophy grid, then **Generate** to suggest names, and a currency from the country's name, that fit it. In Key facts, **Relationship** makes a country a Dominion, Client State or Colony of another: its colour turns into a shade of the other's on the Political view, and choosing None puts it back.
