# Han China AD 100: sources for the country key facts

Every value below was found by one researcher and confirmed against a source by a second, independent checker. A field no source supported is left blank in the scenario.

## Colour 1: Eastern Han Empire (commanderies)

State: Eastern (Later) Han Empire, core commanderies and provinces.

- **ruler**: Emperor He (Liu Zhao), Emperor of Han. Check: verified. Evidence: Hou Hanshu juan 4 opened: He, Emperor Zhang's fourth son, took the throne at ten in Zhanghe 2 (AD 88), reign ran past Yongyuan 12 (AD 100), died at 27 in Yuanxing 1 (AD 106). Reign 88-105/106 covers AD 100. Cited: Hou Hanshu (Book of the Later Han), Annals 4, Xiao He Xiao Shang di ji, Fan Ye (5th c.), https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/後漢書/卷4, opening: 孝和皇帝諱肈，肅宗第四子也; 章和二年二月壬辰，即皇帝位，年十歲; Yongyuan year entries run to the 12th year; death at 27 in the Yuanxing era

- **government**: Hereditary imperial monarchy with a bureaucracy. Check: verified. Evidence: Brill, Administrative Structure of Later Han, opened (first 100k chars): emperor's power described as all but absolute; Three Excellencies and Nine Ministers ran administration; commanderies governed by centrally appointed Administrators. Cited: The Administrative Structure of Later Han (Brill, Chinese Reference Shelf, Dictionary of the Three Kingdoms/Later Han prelims), Rafe de Crespigny, https://ll-chinesereferenceshelf.brillonline.com/dictionary-han/prelims/sim-208574, sections 'The Imperial Power' (authority 'all but absolute'), 'Excellencies, Ministers and other senior officers', 'The Provinces' (commandery ruled by an Administrator)

- **currency**: Wuzhu cash. Check: verified; copper is implied, not stated, in the opened sources. Evidence: Hou Hanshu juan 24 opened: Ma Yuan's memorial to recast wuzhu coins was accepted (c. AD 35-41). Juan 72 opened: Dong Zhuo destroyed the standard wuzhu coins for small ones (c. AD 189-190). Wuzhu was thus the standard between, covering AD 100. Copper is implied by the bronze melted for coinage; coin dealer pages (not relied on) also state wuzhu was cast from AD 41. Cited: Hou Hanshu, juan 24, Ma Yuan biography (Ma Yuan liezhuan), Fan Ye (5th c.), https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/後漢書/卷24, 援 in Longxi memorialised to cast 五銖錢 as of old; 帝從之，天下賴其便; and Hou Hanshu juan 72, Dong Zhuo: 又壞五銖錢，更鑄小錢 (AD 190, ending the wuzhu standard)

- **demonym**: Han. Check: verified. Evidence: Cited juan 90 passage opened: Cai Yong memorial under Emperor Ling (c. AD 177) uses Han people for subjects of Han. Juan 89 opened: Han people/Han subjects (漢人, 漢民) used in the Southern Xiongnu rebellion of AD 109-110, close to AD 100. Supports 'Han' as the people's name in the period. Cited: Hou Hanshu, juan 90, Wuhuan Xianbei liezhuan, Xianbei section (Cai Yong memorial), Fan Ye (5th c.), https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/後漢書/卷90, 漢人逋逃，為之謀主 ('Han people', subjects of the Han state)
