# Spanish Empire 1600: sources for the country key facts

Every value below was found by one researcher and confirmed against a source by a second, independent checker. A field no source supported is left blank in the scenario.

## Colour 1: Spain (Crown of Castile and Aragon)

State: Crown of Castile and Aragon (Spanish Monarchy, Iberian kingdoms).

- **ruler**: Philip III, King of Spain. Check: verified. Evidence: Habsburger.net page: Philip is king of Spain as Philip III and king of Portugal as Philip II, both from 1598, so he reigns in 1600. Cited: Philip III (Habsburger.net person page), Habsburger.net (Austrian Habsburg history portal), https://www.habsburger.net/en/persons/habsburg/philip-iii, page text: 'king of Spain as Philip III from 1598; king of Portugal as Philip II from 1598'

- **government**: Monarchy claiming absolute power, ruled via a favourite. Check: verified. Evidence: Feros introduction (PDF text extracted): Philip III's reign advanced the creation of an absolute monarchy; Lerma was openly the king's sole favourite (privado), from 1598. Cited: Kingship and Favoritism in the Spain of Philip III, 1598-1621 (Introduction, sample chapter), Antonio Feros, Cambridge University Press, 2000; excerpt https://assets.cambridge.org/97805215/61136/excerpt/9780521561136_excerpt.pdf, Introduction, paragraphs on the central argument and on Lerma as sole privado

- **currency**: Silver real; accounts in maravedis (1 real = 34). Check: verified. Evidence: EH.Net review of Hamilton: silver real struck 1497-1686, money-of-account value 34 maravedis; piece of eight 272; coinage not debased in that span. Cited: Classic review of Earl J. Hamilton, American Treasure and the Price Revolution in Spain, 1501-1650, John Munro, EH.Net (Economic History Association) https://eh.net/book_reviews/american-treasure-and-the-price-revolution-in-spain-1501-1650/, passages on the real (34 maravedis), the piece of eight (272 maravedis) and the unchanged gold and silver coinage; page not paginated (approximate locator)

- **demonym**: Spaniard. Check: verified. Evidence: Dictionary.com: a Spaniard is a person from Spain; the usual term for an individual. Cited: 'Spaniard' (dictionary entry), Dictionary.com (Random House), https://www.dictionary.com/browse/spaniard, headword entry

## Colour 3: Spanish Indies (Americas and Philippines)

State: Spanish Indies (Viceroyalties of New Spain and Peru, with the Philippines).

- **ruler**: Philip III, King of Spain and the New World. Check: verified. Evidence: HAB engraved portrait legend styles Philip III king of the Spains and the New World; Habsburger.net gives his Spanish reign from 1598. Cited: Engraved portrait of Philip III, record A 28135 (inv. I 12749.3), 'Figura XV', from a book, Herzog August Bibliothek Wolfenbuettel (Portraits collection), https://dev.hab.de/portraits/werk/27544/xml/, oval legend 'PHILIPPVS III. DEI GRATIA HISPANIARVM AC NOVI ORBIS REX POTENTISS.'

- **government**: Crown dominions under viceroys; no Cortes. Check: verified. Evidence: Cardim article: no Cortes was ever convened in New Spain or Peru; a Peruvian viceroy called the king more absolute there; lesser juntas only, under royal control. Cited: Representative Institutions and Parliamentary Culture in the Portuguese and Spanish Empires (c. 1500-c. 1700), Pedro Cardim, Transactions of the Royal Historical Society, vol. 3 (2025), pp. 479-498; https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/transactions-of-the-royal-historical-society/article/representative-institutions-and-parliamentary-culture-in-the-portuguese-and-spanish-empires-c-1500c-1700/F21F4E68DCE923AE9E714057D6346F81, sections on Spanish America (no Cortes; Council of the Indies; viceroy calls the king 'more absolute') (approximate locator)

- **currency**: Silver peso (real de a ocho), 272 maravedis. Check: verified. Evidence: Lamikiz footnote: 'peso' means the silver peso worth 272 maravedis; EH.Net review: piece of eight (8 reales) valued at 272 maravedis from 1497. Cited: The fiscal transformation of the Spanish Carrera de Indias in the 17th century: a reinterpretation, Xabier Lamikiz, Revista de Historia Economica - Journal of Iberian and Latin American Economic History 42(2), 2024, pp. 171-192; https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/revista-de-historia-economica-journal-of-iberian-and-latin-american-economic-history/article/fiscal-transformation-of-the-spanish-carrera-de-indias-in-the-17th-century-a-reinterpretation/F65D19D3EC5832C85AD26C15D095183F, definition of 'peso' as the silver peso worth 272 maravedis (units note) (approximate locator)

- **demonym**: Spanish. Check: verified. Evidence: Dictionary.com: Spanish, as a noun, the Spanish people; adjective, relating to Spain or its people. Cited: 'Spanish' (dictionary entry), Dictionary.com (Random House), https://www.dictionary.com/browse/spanish, headword entry

## Colour 4: Kingdom of Portugal

State: Kingdom of Portugal (in dynastic union with Spain since 1580).

- **ruler**: Philip II of Portugal (Philip III of Spain), King. Check: verified. Evidence: Habsburger.net page: Philip is king of Spain as Philip III and king of Portugal as Philip II, both from 1598, so he reigns in 1600. Cited: Philip III (Habsburger.net person page), Habsburger.net (Austrian Habsburg history portal), https://www.habsburger.net/en/persons/habsburg/philip-iii, page text: 'king of Spain as Philip III from 1598; king of Portugal as Philip II from 1598'

- **government**: Kingdom in dynastic union with Spain, keeping its own laws. Check: verified. Evidence: Brasilhis entry: under the Tomar pacts Portugal stayed governed by its own laws and institutions, with a Portuguese-only Council of Portugal, within the Hispanic Monarchy. Cited: Council of Portugal, in BRASILHIS Dictionary: Biographic and Thematic Dictionary of Brazil in the Spanish Monarchy (1580-1640), Sergio Moreta Pedraz, Ediciones Universidad de Salamanca, https://brasilhisdictionary.usal.es/en/consejo-de-portugal-3/, entry text: Tomar pacts; 'Form of union and rule'

- **currency**: Real (plural reis). Check: verified. Evidence: Topoi abstract: Philips kept Portuguese currency and real's exchange rate stable (says 'real' only). BCB Museu de Valores page gives Portuguese coin values in Reis, the real as unit. Cited: Uma pax monetaria? Impasses do sistema monetario portugues no tempo dos Filipes (1580-1640), Pedro Puntoni, Topoi. Revista de Historia 21(43), 2020; https://revistas.ufrj.br/index.php/topoi/article/view/70936, abstract

- **demonym**: Portuguese. Check: verified. Evidence: Dictionary.com: a Portuguese is a native or inhabitant of Portugal. Cited: 'Portuguese' (dictionary entry), Dictionary.com (Random House), https://www.dictionary.com/browse/portuguese, headword entry

## Colour 5: Portuguese Empire (Brazil and Asian bases)

State: Portuguese overseas possessions (Brazil, Atlantic and Asian bases such as Goa), under the Portuguese crown.

- **ruler**: Philip II of Portugal (Philip III of Spain), King. Check: verified. Evidence: Habsburger.net page: Philip is king of Spain as Philip III and king of Portugal as Philip II, both from 1598, so he reigns in 1600. Cited: Philip III (Habsburger.net person page), Habsburger.net (Austrian Habsburg history portal), https://www.habsburger.net/en/persons/habsburg/philip-iii, page text: 'king of Spain as Philip III from 1598; king of Portugal as Philip II from 1598'

- **government**: Portuguese overseas lands, kept separate from Spain's. Check: verified. Evidence: Brasilhis entry: Philip II kept Portuguese and Castilian overseas possessions separate, so Portuguese America retained its own character. Cited: Council of Portugal, in BRASILHIS Dictionary: Biographic and Thematic Dictionary of Brazil in the Spanish Monarchy (1580-1640), Sergio Moreta Pedraz, Ediciones Universidad de Salamanca, https://brasilhisdictionary.usal.es/en/consejo-de-portugal-3/, entry text: Tomar pacts; 'Form of union and rule'

- **currency**: Real (plural reis), the Portuguese unit of account. Check: verified. Evidence: BCB Museu de Valores: Portuguese coins in colonial Brazil matched those of Portugal, valued in Reis; the Real was the monetary unit. Topoi abstract confirms the real under the Philips. Cited: Money in Brazil - from Discovery to the United Kingdom, Banco Central do Brasil (Museu de Valores), https://www.bcb.gov.br/ingles/odinheiroi.asp?frame=1, opening section on colonial money: Portuguese coin values 'expressed in Reis'

- **demonym**: Portuguese. Check: verified. Evidence: Dictionary.com: a Portuguese is a native or inhabitant of Portugal. Cited: 'Portuguese' (dictionary entry), Dictionary.com (Random House), https://www.dictionary.com/browse/portuguese, headword entry
