# Ottoman Empire 1683: sources for the country key facts

Every value below was found by one researcher and confirmed against a source by a second, independent checker. A field no source supported is left blank in the scenario.

## Colour 1: Ottoman Empire (Anatolia, Balkans, Hungary)

State: Ottoman Empire, core (Anatolia, Balkans, Ottoman Hungary).

- **ruler**: Mehmed IV, Sultan. Check: verified. Evidence: Cleveland catalogue entry: tughra of Sultan Mehmed IV, dated 1648-87, so he reigned in 1683. Same sultan rules all Ottoman provinces. Cited: Tughra: Shah Muhammad bin Ibrahim Khan, al-muzaffar daima (Sultan Mehmed, son of Ibrahim Khan, the eternally victorious), accession 1944.525, Cleveland Museum of Art (catalogue entry), https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1944.525, Catalogue entry: title, date 1648-87, description

- **government**: Hereditary sultanate, absolute in theory. Check: verified. Evidence: LoC study: sultan theoretically answerable only to God (absolute in theory, limited in practice). Same study's Ottoman Empire chapter: one Osman dynasty, 36 sultans over six centuries. Cited: Turkey: A Country Study, 'Ottoman Institutions', Library of Congress, Federal Research Division, https://countrystudies.us/turkey/7.htm, Section 'Ottoman Institutions', paragraph on the sultan's powers

- **motto**: al-Muzaffar daima (the eternally victorious). Check: verified. Evidence: Cleveland entry: Mehmed IV's tughra reads 'Mehmed, son of Ibrahim Khan, the Eternally Victorious' (al-muzaffar daima in title). It is his tughra formula. Cited: Tughra: Shah Muhammad bin Ibrahim Khan, al-muzaffar daima (Sultan Mehmed, son of Ibrahim Khan, the eternally victorious), accession 1944.525, Cleveland Museum of Art (catalogue entry), https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1944.525, Catalogue entry: title, date 1648-87, description

- **demonym**: Ottoman. Check: verified. Evidence: LoC study opens with Osman's line, which the West calls the Ottomans. Cited: Turkey: A Country Study, 'The Ottoman Empire', Library of Congress, Federal Research Division, https://countrystudies.us/turkey/6.htm, Opening paragraphs: Osmanli dynasty 'better known in the West as the Ottomans'

## Colour 3: Ottoman North African regencies

State: North African regencies (Algiers, Tripoli, Tunis).

- **government**: Ottoman regency under local military rule (Algiers). Check: unsupported; trimmed to what the check confirmed (claimed: Semi-autonomous Ottoman regencies under local military rule). Evidence: Roughton thesis abstract (opened) covers Algeria only: sultan recognised as suzerain, Janissaries held political control, dey from 1671. LoC Algeria study agrees for Algiers. No opened source covers Tunis or Tripoli in 1683 (Britannica, GlobalSecurity not openable). Cited: The Administration of Ottoman Algeria (1517-1830), Richard Allen Roughton, M.A. thesis (History), University of Maryland, 1962; http://hdl.handle.net/1903/25225, Chapter 1 (PDF p. 4): Sultan recognised as suzerain; Janissaries held political control; Deylik instituted 1671

## Colour 4: Crimean Khanate

State: Crimean Khanate (Danubian principalities painted with it).

- **ruler**: Murad Giray, Khan (deposed after Vienna, 1683). Check: verified. Evidence: Yalvar article (text extracted): Murad Giray khan 1678-1683, deposed after the failed Vienna siege; Haci II Giray then accepted the khanate. Klein contents also give his reign as 1678-1683. Cited: Suclamalarin Golgesinde Bir Kirim Hani: Murad Giray Han, Cihan Yalvar, Yeditepe Universitesi Tarih Bolumu Arastirma Dergisi (YUTAD) 3(6), 16 July 2019; https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/762463, Section 4 (PDF pp. 16-17): Murad Giray deposed after the failed siege; Haci II Giray accepted the khanate

- **government**: Giray khanate under Ottoman suzerainty. Check: verified. Evidence: Klein introduction: Crimean Khanate was a semi-autonomous polity under Ottoman suzerainty; khans served under the sultan's command. Yalvar shows the sultan appointing and deposing khans. Cited: Suclamalarin Golgesinde Bir Kirim Hani: Murad Giray Han, Cihan Yalvar, Yeditepe Universitesi Tarih Bolumu Arastirma Dergisi (YUTAD) 3(6), 16 July 2019; https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/762463, Section 4 (PDF pp. 16-17): Murad Giray deposed after the failed siege; Haci II Giray accepted the khanate

- **demonym**: Crimean Tatar. Check: verified. Evidence: Klein volume contents and introduction use 'Crimean Tatars' and 'Crimean Tatar elite' for the khanate's people. Cited: The Crimean Khanate between East and West (15th-18th Century), Ed. Denise Klein, Harrassowitz Verlag, Wiesbaden, 2012; https://www.harrassowitz-verlag.de/dzo/artikel/201/001/1503_201.pdf, Contents page (PDF p. 2): chapter title 'Captured by the Crimean Tatars in 1657'
