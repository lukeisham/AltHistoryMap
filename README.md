# AltHistoryMap

An app made for my son Solomon.

A zoomable world map for building alternative histories: create imaginary maps, flags and crests, and save them. Found cities, plant flags and paint territory, or start from one of the ready-made empire sketches and change it.

## Open it
Visit [althistorymap.pages.dev/app](https://althistorymap.pages.dev/app/) in a current browser. It is also at [lukeisham.github.io/AltHistoryMap](https://lukeisham.github.io/AltHistoryMap/). Nothing is sent anywhere: your map stays on your computer.

## Use it
- Pick a starting map from **Scenarios** at the bottom, or begin with the blank world.
- **Political**, **Province** and **Culture** each show one layer of the map. Keys 1, 2 and 3 switch between them.
- Switch to **Edit** to paint, fill, draw borders and place cities, flags, symbols and labels. Undo with Ctrl/Cmd-Z.
- **Cities by nation** colours each city's dot by the nation that holds it.
- **Save** downloads your map as a file; **Open** (or dropping the file on the page) loads it again. **PNG** saves a picture.
- **Flags** are drawn in the flag designer: pick any colour with the **+** swatch, or stamp a crest onto the flag. Rest the pointer on a flag to see it larger.
- **Crests** are built in the crest maker from shield shapes, divisions, ordinaries and charges. Both open from the panel on the left in Edit mode, and both have **Save PNG**.
